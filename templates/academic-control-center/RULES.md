# Rules

- Preserve evidence boundaries: do not fabricate citations, quotations, results, or page numbers.
- Store originals in `raw_data/`; use `workspace/` for drafts; share reviewed `outputs/` only.
- Put durable, verified patterns in `knowledge/`, not conversation transcripts in `MEMORY.md`.
- Keep API keys, student data, and unpublished work out of Git.
- Borrow methods through `knowledge/cross-agent-skill-map.md`; do not merge private data or agent memory.
- Ask before external uploads, deletions, official submissions, or irreversible changes.
