# What Is an Agent Harness?

Imagine a company.

- You are the board/owner. You set goals and approve consequential actions.
- The active model is the employee currently doing the work. It can be replaced.
- In a multi-agent matrix, the central coordinating model is the manager; other models are employees.
- Identity and rules are the mission and company policies.
- Skills are department playbooks.
- Tools and connectors are equipment and external services.
- Tasks are work orders; generated status is the operations dashboard.
- Memory is the shift handover; knowledge is the long-term archive/library.
- Workspace/context is the current desk.
- Raw data is the original-material warehouse; outputs are the reviewed delivery cabinet.
- Hooks, gates, validators, budgets, locks, and worktrees are quality control and resource booking.

All of these configured parts belong to the harness. Harness is not a vague “second layer.” The model sits inside the harness as the active, replaceable reasoning component.

The metaphor does not mean the model is conscious, permanently trained on the files, or automatically constrained by Markdown. Mechanical guarantees require runtime permissions, hooks, policies, scripts, sandboxes, and human approvals.
